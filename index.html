Photo Logger

Your department's clinical photos, in one place

A phone app for Android and iPhone, used by clinical departments to photograph patients and keep the photos in one shared record.

Photo Logger is a work tool for hospital staff, not a public app. To use it you need an invite code from your department's admin.

What it does

  • Scan the OPD case sheet. The phone reads the patient's UHID and name from a photo of the sheet. The reading happens on the phone, and the sheet photo is deleted once the details are confirmed.
  • Take clinical photos. Photos are filed under the patient, in a dated session, with the patient's name and UHID shown while you take them.
  • One shared record. Everyone in the department sees the same patients and photos, and can find them by UHID or name.
  • Compare and share. Make before/after collages, and export a patient's photos as a PDF to send where you choose.

Signing in

Members sign in with their Google account and then join their department with its invite code. Signing in again on a new phone brings you back to your department.

Privacy

Photo Logger handles clinical photographs and patient identifiers. Read the privacy policy to see what is collected, where it is stored, and who can see it.
