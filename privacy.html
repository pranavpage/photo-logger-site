Photo Logger

Privacy policy

Last updated 8 October 2026

In short: Photo Logger keeps a hospital department's clinical photos and the patient details they are filed under. Only members of that department can see them. The data is stored with our hosting provider, Supabase, in Mumbai, India, and on the phones of department members. It is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone else. Google, or Apple on iPhone, is used only to sign you in.

Who this covers

Photo Logger is used by hospital departments, such as a dermatology OPD. The department decides what is photographed and recorded, and is responsible for having patients' consent. Photo Logger's developer runs the app and its server on the department's behalf. The app is for hospital staff only.

What is collected

About patients, entered by department members:

  • UHID and name.
  • Clinical photographs, taken with the phone's camera or added from its photo library, and before/after collages made from them.
  • The date and time of each photo session, and any notes written for it.

About members (the staff who use the app):

  • From Google or Apple, when you sign in: your name, email address and account ID. With Apple you can choose to hide your email, in which case Apple gives a private relay address. Google also provides a link to your profile picture; the app does not use it.
  • The name you choose to show colleagues, your role (admin or member), and when you joined or were removed.
  • A record of who did what: who registered each patient, who took or added each photo and started each session, and who deleted a photo and when.

Not collected: the photo of the OPD case sheet you scan. The UHID and name are read from it on your phone, and the photo is deleted once you confirm or retake it. It is never uploaded. The app has no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

Why

Only to provide the app: to keep each patient's photos in one record that the department can find later, to show who took and changed what, and to sign you in. Nothing is used for any other purpose.

Where it is stored

  • The department's shared record is stored with Supabase, our hosting provider, in its Mumbai, India data centre. Photos are kept in private storage that only department members can read.
  • On members' phones. Photos taken on a phone are kept on it, and colleagues' photos are copied to it so the record works without a connection. They are kept in the app's private storage on the phone. Protect your phone with a screen lock.

Data travels between the app and the server over encrypted (HTTPS) connections.

Who can see it

  • Only signed-in members of the same department. This is enforced by the server, not just by the app. Members of one department cannot see another department's record.
  • A department admin can remove a member; their access to the shared record ends straight away.
  • Supabase stores the data for us and does not use it for its own purposes. Google and Apple receive only what they need to sign you in.
  • When you export a PDF or share a photo or collage, it goes wherever you send it. That copy is then outside Photo Logger.

We do not sell data or share it with anyone else, except where the law requires it.

How long it is kept, and deleting it

  • The record is kept while the department uses Photo Logger.
  • When a photo is deleted in the app, it disappears from every phone, but the server keeps it with a note of who deleted it and when. This keeps the clinical record complete and shows what changed. It can be removed permanently on request.
  • Removing a member does not remove the photos they took; those belong to the department's record.
  • A department, a member, or a patient (through the department) can ask for their data to be deleted. We will delete it unless the department has to keep it as part of its clinical records.

Contact

For anything about a department's record, start with that department's admin. To reach the developer, use the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen for Photo Logger.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
